# Dog-adoption mortality-claim curation

- Promoted Johnson’s August 20 dog-adoption post as the batch’s lead new signal: the highest-engagement post of the August 18–20 window (12,850 likes at capture) announcing a Belgian Malinois named Katara, paired with the claim that dog owners have about a 24% lower risk of dying early.
- Added source-aware literature context rather than repeating the number uncited: the 24% figure matches an unadjusted 2019 meta-analysis of 10 observational studies and roughly 3.8 million participants (Kramer et al., *Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes*), and a 2021 reappraisal using confounder-adjusted estimates found the all-cause mortality association statistically nonsignificant (pooled RR ≈ 0.93). Both papers are cited on the signal and opinion entries.
- Kept the item in the attributed-claim lane: observational association, not a demonstrated causal effect, not medical advice, and not a reason to adopt a dog for health.
