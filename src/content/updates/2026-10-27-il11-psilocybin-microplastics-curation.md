- Promoted the IL-11 lifespan relay with its transcription drift documented: Johnson quote-posted Ryan Bethencourt’s same-age-mice photo with “Extends the medium lifespan of male mice by 22.5% and 35% in female mice… I posted about it in 2024… It is now in Phase II human clinical trials for lung fibrosis” (1,048 likes, 51 reposts, 43 replies). Verified: Widjaja et al., Nature, July 17, 2024 (10.1038/s41586-024-07701-9) — anti-IL-11 (X203) from 75 weeks until death extended median lifespan 22.5% in males (130.3→159.6 weeks) and 25% in females (117.1→146.4 weeks), with fewer tumours at autopsy and the ERK–AMPK–mTORC1 aging-axis framing; Boehringer Ingelheim’s January 13, 2026 announcement of the BI 765423 Phase IIa IPF trial (asset in-licensed from the same Duke-NUS/SingHealth lineage); and Lassen Therapeutics’ LASN01 anti-IL-11-receptor Phase 1/2a completion in pulmonary fibrosis (NCT05331300). Drift recorded: the paper’s female figure is 25%, not 35%, and Johnson’s own July 17, 2024 post (1813710102308028577) quotes both figures correctly — verified via live Bird search — so the October 26 relay degrades against both the paper and his own earlier post, the same drift class as the October 24 bone-pressure figure.
- Promoted the psilocybin-Parkinson’s relay with its dropped limitations: “Another win for psilocybin. In 9 Parkinson’s patients… GDF15 fell > 17.4% after 10 mg > 13.9% after 25 mg… IL-6: −17% > IL-8: −20% > hsCRP: −9.6% > IL-10: +2.7%” (1,741 likes, 180 reposts, 79 replies). Verified: Bradley, Kelley, et al., JAMA Psychiatry research letter, published October 21, 2026 (10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2026.2733) — secondary analysis of banked blood from 9 of 12 participants in the UCSF open-label pilot (NCT04932434; parent trial Bradley et al., Neuropsychopharmacology 2025, 10.1038/s41386-025-02097-0, 12 participants, 10 mg then 25 mg with psychotherapy); five inflammatory markers fell and stayed significantly below baseline a month after the final dose; GDF15 improved sharply right after dosing then lost the gain by longer follow-up. Boundaries recorded: single-arm, no control, unblinded; n=9 banked samples; markers move with sleep/stress/infection/mood; psychedelic-industry co-author ties; the authors call it an initial biological signal needing controlled confirmation — “another win” is a scoreboard frame the letter does not support.
- Promoted the microplastics-reduction checklist with item-level verification: “how to reduce microplastics > glass or steel water bottle > never microwave food in plastic > wood cutting board > reverse osmosis water filter > loose leaf tea with stainless mesh” (4,855 likes, 190 reposts, 215 replies). Verified per item: microwave-plastic release up to 4.22M microplastics + 2.11B nanoplastics per cm² in 3 min (ES&T 2023, 10.1021/acs.est.3c04542s); plastic-cutting-board shedding 7.4–50.7 g/person-year (Chen et al., ES&T 2023, 10.1021/acs.est.3c00924); plastic-teabag release ~11.6B microplastics/cup (Hernandez et al., ES&T 2019, 10.1021/acs.est.9b02540) plus the October 13, 2026 head-to-head of 555–2,498 particles/cup (bagged) vs 0.3–2.4 (bulk/loose leaf; Pehlivan & Gedik, npj Science of Food, 10.1038/s41538-026-01175-x). Boundary recorded: exposure-reduction direction traces, but no study yet demonstrates reducing these exposures improves human health outcomes; the checklist names no study.
