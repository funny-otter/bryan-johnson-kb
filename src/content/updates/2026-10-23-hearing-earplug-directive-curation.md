- Promoted the hearing-preservation directive: “Take earplugs everywhere / Environments routinely above 85 dBA / > restaurants / > sporting events / > social gatherings / > gym and theater / > trains and subways / > coffee shops / Can’t currently fix hearing loss. / Hearing loss accelerates brain volume loss and cognitive decline.” (2,034 likes, 171 replies, 94 reposts). The medical claims verify in direction: Lin et al., NeuroImage 2014 (10.1016/j.neuroimage.2013.12.059) followed 126 BLSA adults aged 56–86 for a mean 6.4 years and found hearing impairment independently associated with accelerated whole-brain atrophy (8.4 vs 7.2 cm³/year) plus faster right-temporal decline; and the 2024 Lancet Commission ranks hearing loss among its 14 modifiable dementia risk factors (HR 1.37, n=666,370; hearing aids associated with 17% lower dementia risk; ACHIEVE RCT’s 48% protective effect confined to the high-risk ARIC subgroup). The boundary recorded: the “routinely above 85 dBA” venue list overstates the measured record for the everyday venues named — NYC transit dosimetry averages 80.4 dBA on subway platforms with only ~20% of measurements over 85, and the 2,376-venue SoundPrint restaurant survey averages 77–78 dBA with 25% above 81 — and 85 dBA is an 8-hour workplace time-weighted limit, not a momentary danger line; no trial shows earplugs prevent cognitive decline, and the post names no study.
