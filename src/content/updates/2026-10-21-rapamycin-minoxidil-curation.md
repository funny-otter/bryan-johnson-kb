I personally take 3.5 mg daily” (2,677 likes, 140 replies, 41 reposts).
- Verified both numbers: the 36.4 is the twice-daily arm of Veradermics’ Phase 3 ‘304’ topline (536 men; 28.6 QD / 36.4 BID vs 6.6 placebo hairs/cm² at month 6, p<0.0001; no treatment-related SAEs, no cardiac AESIs), and the 35.9 traces to Panchaprateep and Luengarun’s open-label study of 30 men on 5 mg once daily for 24 weeks — the only published 5-mg hair-density dataset, as a JAMA Dermatology trial noted when citing it.
