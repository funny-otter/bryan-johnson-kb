# Aviation, checklists, and the Autonomous Self origin story

- Promoted Johnson’s July 28 aviation essay because it gives durable context for why Blueprint is framed as an automated control system rather than a collection of daily choices.
- Summarized his account that flight training’s checklists, procedures, and active risk management inspired a morning-authored food cutoff and later the “Autonomous Self,” Blueprint, and Don’t Die.
- Treated the essay as autobiographical and source-stated. It does not show that the food cutoff caused the reported sleep, weight, fatigue, or energy changes; it does not establish a treatment for depression or eating disorders; and it is not reader meal-timing advice.
- Added one source-aware signal, one timeline event, and concise context to the Bryan Johnson, Blueprint Protocol, and Don’t Die knowledge pages.
