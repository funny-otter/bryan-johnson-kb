- Verified the outcome literature: the CMAJ 2016 meta-analysis of 46 prospective cohorts (Zhang, Shen, Qi; 10.1503/cmaj.150535; 1,246,203 adults, 78,349 deaths) found each 10 beats/min higher resting heart rate associated with all-cause mortality RR 1.09 (95% CI 1.07–1.12) and cardiovascular mortality RR 1.08, with RHR >80 bpm at RR 1.45 for all-cause mortality, a linear dose-response from the 45 bpm reference, and independence from traditional risk factors — but also substantial heterogeneity, detected publication bias (trim-and-fill corrected all-cause RR 1.04), and the authors note resting heart rate is not a measurement recommended in American or European cardiovascular risk-assessment guidelines.
