Verification against the publisher page: dynamic tibial axial loading (DTAL; 2 N at 2 Hz, 300 cycles, twice weekly) in mice at 20–24 months of age extended median lifespan by 9.4%/79.5 days (mean +11.9% females, +7.7% males; maximum +7.6%/+15.4%), reversed bone marrow immunosenescence, reduced liver steatosis, and improved cognition and endurance, with osteocyte-Piezo1 deletion accelerating whole-body aging and PEDF identified as a novel mechano-responsive anti-aging osteokine — covering the brain/liver/immune triad the original post named. The correction’s PGE2 mechanism sentence traces to the same research group’s prior mechanism work: loading-induced prostaglandin E2 release through Cx43 hemichannels promotes bone formation and reduces resorption (Zhao et al., eLife 2022, 10.7554/eLife.74365).
